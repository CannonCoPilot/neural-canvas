neural-canvas

A personal art docent for a living-room TV.

neural-canvas is a small program that runs on its owner's own computer. Given a photograph of a painting, print or drawing, it works out which artwork it is, frames it for a 4K screen, and adds a short museum-style label: title, artist, date, and a paragraph about the work.

It then places the finished frame in one Google Photos album, which the owner's TV shows in its ambient mode, so the screen becomes a rotating gallery.

How it uses Google Photos

neural-canvas asks for the narrowest Google Photos permissions that let it do this job:

  • Add photos: upload each finished frame and create the album it goes in.
  • See what it created: find that album and the frames it uploaded.
  • Edit what it created: keep the album's title, and the frames in it, as the owner configures.

It cannot see, change or delete any photo or album it did not create itself.

Who it is for

neural-canvas is a personal project, used by its owner on their own account. It is not offered as a service to anyone else.
