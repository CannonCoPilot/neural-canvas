Privacy policy

neural-canvas · effective 4 November 2026

neural-canvas is a personal program that runs on its owner's own computer. This policy explains what it accesses and what it does with it.

What it accesses

  • Artwork images that the owner places in a folder on their computer.
  • Google Photos, through the Google Photos Library API, with three permissions: add photos and create an album; see the photos and albums it created; edit the photos and albums it created. It cannot see or change anything else in the account.

What it does with it

  • Images are processed on the owner's computer, with models that run on that computer.
  • To identify an artwork, an image may be compared with public museum and encyclopaedia collections and searched for on the public web, as a person would with a reverse image search.
  • The finished frame and its label are uploaded to the owner's own Google Photos album.

What it never does

  • It does not sell, rent or share data with anyone.
  • It does not use data for advertising.
  • It does not send Google user data to any server other than Google's.
  • It does not read any photo or album it did not create.

Google API Services

neural-canvas's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Google user data, item by item

  • Album it creates: its identifier and title, kept on the owner's computer so frames go to the same album every time.
  • Frames it uploads: their identifiers and links, kept in the run record on the owner's computer so each artwork is delivered only once.
  • Sign-in token: issued by Google, kept on the owner's computer.

neural-canvas does not collect names, email addresses, contacts, location, or any photo it did not create. It has no server of its own, so none of this data ever leaves the owner's computer except to Google itself.

Retention and deletion

Album and frame identifiers are kept for as long as the owner uses neural-canvas, and are deleted when the owner deletes the program's folder. Frames it uploaded stay in Google Photos until the owner deletes them there.

Security

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer, readable only by the owner's user account, and is never written to logs or shared. All traffic to Google uses HTTPS.

Your control

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections. After that, neural-canvas can no longer reach the account.

Children

neural-canvas is a personal tool, not directed at children, and collects no data from anyone but its owner.

Changes to this policy

Any change will be published on this page, with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/CannonCoPilot/neural-canvas/issues.
